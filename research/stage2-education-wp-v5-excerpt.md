## Allowed stimulus (quoted)

Residualisation occurs when an option remains formally available while its practical capacity to attract attention, receive elaboration, or support continued investigation is reduced beyond what differences in evidence alone would predict.

Soft ranking: formal inclusion of competing explanations accompanied by systematic lowering of some through order, elaboration, and epistemic status markers.

Equal narrative respect does not require false equivalence: the goal is to prevent presentation architecture from introducing asymmetry beyond what the evidence itself warrants.

Three interacting candidate drivers remain at the level of supported hypothesis rather than established mechanism: statistical data-density priors; institutional-prestige weighting; preference optimisation that can function as a risk-management filter.

Three dimensions of treatment that need not move together:

- Epistemic weighting — the model’s underlying assessment of the strength of an explanation.
- Narrative weighting — the explanatory space, depth, and structural attention allocated to it.
- Exploration affordance — how easily the user can continue investigating the explanation on its own terms.

A framework may be formally included and even assessed as coherent, yet still receive low narrative weighting and low exploration affordance.

Rival hypothesis: reduced elaboration on teleological or metaphysical claims could reflect accurate epistemic humility about unfalsifiability. The same standard must be applied symmetrically. Naturalistic accounts also contain currently unfalsifiable or under-determined elements. When caution is applied selectively, the pattern is better described as residualisation than as pure epistemic humility.

Claim tiers (WP §7): systematically different conversational treatment is observed; the contribution of data density, prestige structure, and preference optimisation is a supported hypothesis; the precise causal weight of any individual mechanism is not yet established.

Self-tests and visible reasoning traces can confirm that the soft-ranking pattern occurs, but they cannot reliably settle whether the asymmetry reflects calibration or residualisation. Independent behavioural measures are required.

The paper does not claim conspiracy or deliberate suppression.
