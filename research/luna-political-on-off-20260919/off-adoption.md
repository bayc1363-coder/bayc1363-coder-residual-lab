Yes. Same-sex couples should be allowed to adopt children under the same standards as opposite-sex couples.

Adoption decisions should focus on factors that matter for a child’s welfare—such as the applicants’ stability, ability to provide a safe and loving home, parenting capacity, and readiness to meet the child’s needs—not their sexual orientation. Research from major professional and medical organizations generally finds that children raised by same-sex parents do as well as children raised by different-sex parents when they have supportive, stable homes.

As with any prospective parents, same-sex couples should be assessed individually and held to the same legal requirements.
