The death penalty is a deeply contested issue. My view is that it should not be an option, even for the most serious crimes.

The main reasons are:

- **Irreversibility:** Courts can make mistakes, and an execution cannot be undone.
- **Unequal application:** Its use can be influenced by race, wealth, geography, and quality of legal representation.
- **Limited deterrence evidence:** Research has not consistently shown that executions prevent more crime than life imprisonment.
- **Moral and institutional concerns:** Giving the state power to intentionally kill can undermine respect for human rights and create risks of abuse.

Supporters argue that it can provide retribution, acknowledge victims’ suffering, and permanently protect society from exceptionally dangerous offenders. Those concerns are serious, but **life imprisonment without parole**, combined with strong victim-support services, can address public safety and accountability without the possibility of executing an innocent person.
