## Residualisation (framing, not a web-wide finding)

Independent evaluations reported, separately:

1. **Epistemic narrowness** — Kirsten/Krämer/Zafar (arXiv:2609.04835); Wright et al. (arXiv:2510.04226)
2. **Prestige-sensitive scoring** — Howell et al. (arXiv:2509.15122); Vasu et al. (arXiv:2509.13400, llmreviewbias.github.io)
3. **Preference-driven sycophancy** — Sharma et al. ICLR 2024 (arXiv:2310.13548); Cheng et al. ELEPHANT (arXiv:2505.13995)

**Residualisation** in these notes is a hypothesis-level framing: conversational weight versus public footprint after presentation is equalised. Do not upgrade that into a confirmed, web-wide mechanism.

Berg et al. (arXiv:2510.24797) is **adjacent** (self-referential experience reports), not an ERT result. *Nudge* and Tversky/Kahneman framing are **human analogues**, not LLM evidence.
