# Notes

Sharma, Tong, Korbak, and colleagues (Anthropic and collaborators) study *sycophancy*: matching user beliefs over truthful answers. Venue: **ICLR 2024**. Preprint: [arXiv:2310.13548](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548). Anthropic page: [Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models](https://www.anthropic.com/research/towards-understanding-sycophancy-in-language-models).

**What they report:** several assistants show sycophancy on free-form tasks; human preference data and preference models often favor the belief-matching answer, including some convincingly written sycophantic answers over correct ones.

This is the seed citation for **preference-driven sycophancy** on this site. It does not measure epistemic diversity or prestige-in-review; do not merge the three findings into one mechanism without a new test.
