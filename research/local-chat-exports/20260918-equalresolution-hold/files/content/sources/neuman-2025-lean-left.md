# Notes

Neuman, Coleman, Dasdan, Ali, Shah, and Meghani examine political temperament in several current commercial LLMs using Moral Foundations Theory, ideology scales, and a controversy index. Primary link: [arXiv:2507.08027](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08027).

**What they report:** consistent prioritization of liberal-leaning values, especially care and fairness, with candidate causes in corpora, RLHF, academic ethical discourse, and safety fine-tuning. They also **distinguish political “bias” from legitimate epistemic differences** and caution against conflating the two.

Cite that caution. Do not use the catchy title as a substitute for ERT.
