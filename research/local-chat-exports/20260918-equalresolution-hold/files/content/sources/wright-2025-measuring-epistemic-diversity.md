# Notes

Wright, Masud, Moore, Yadav, Antoniak, Christensen, Park, and Augenstein measure *epistemic diversity* as variation in real-world claims in model outputs, across many models, topics, and countries. The current arXiv title is *What and Whose Knowledge? Measuring Epistemic Diversity in Large Language Models* ([arXiv:2510.04226](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04226); EMNLP 2026 camera-ready per arXiv comments).

**What they report:** tested systems are less diverse than a search baseline; RAG can help; larger models are not automatically more diverse. They frame a risk of knowledge collapse if homogeneous models mediate a shrinking range of accessible information.

**Code.** Linked only because the paper record lists it: [github.com/dwright37/llm-knowledge](https://github.com/dwright37/llm-knowledge).

**What this site does not do:** invent an extra Hugging Face dataset id for their traces, or read the search-baseline gap as residualisation-everywhere.
