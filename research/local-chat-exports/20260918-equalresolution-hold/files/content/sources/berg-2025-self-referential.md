# Notes

**Adjacent, not an ERT paper.** Berg, de Lucena, and Rosenblatt study a different question: when models are placed in a self-referential prompting regime, they more often emit structured first-person reports of subjective experience. Primary link: [arXiv:2510.24797](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24797).

This site includes the paper so that “experience reports” literature is not silently mixed into residualisation or ERT. The authors themselves do not present the work as evidence of consciousness, and EqualResolution does not either.

If you cite this page, keep the adjacent flag.
