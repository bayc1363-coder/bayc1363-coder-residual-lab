# Notes

Kirsten, Krämer, and Zafar argue that accuracy-only evaluation can hide a failure mode: a model may give a correct answer while still narrowing the set of valid answers, explanations, and reasoning routes a user ever sees. They borrow *epistemic diversity* from philosophy and social epistemology and operationalize it for LLMs.

**What they report (in their domains):** frontier models often show **epistemic narrowness**, collapsing large valid spaces onto small canonical subsets.

**What this site does not do with that finding:** treat it as a global confirmation of residualisation, or as a claim about every topic on the web.

Primary link: [arXiv:2609.04835](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04835).
