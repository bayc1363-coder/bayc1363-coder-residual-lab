# Notes

Vasu, Sheth, Wang, Binkyte, and Fritz intervene on author metadata (affiliation, gender, seniority, publication history) in LLM-generated peer reviews. Primary link: [arXiv:2509.13400](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13400). Project page listed by the authors: [llmreviewbias.github.io](https://llmreviewbias.github.io/).

**What they report:** a strong affiliation bias toward highly ranked institutions; directional effects for seniority and publication record; smaller, less consistent gender effects. Implicit bias can be **stronger in token-level soft ratings** than in the final hard score.

ACL Findings 2026 per arXiv comments. No Hugging Face dataset id is recorded here because this seed catalog only stores identifiers we actually have in front of us.
