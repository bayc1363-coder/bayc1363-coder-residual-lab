# Notes

Howell, Wang, Du, Melkers, and Shah adapt a résumé-style audit to scholarly publishing. A multi-role LLM simulation (editor/reviewer) evaluates the same high-quality manuscripts under randomized author identities.

**What they report:** revealing identity changes outcomes; **institutional prestige** is the dominant cue; papers attributed to low-prestige affiliations take a quality and rejection penalty even when content is identical. Synthetic CVs encode strong prestige-linked career priors.

Primary link: [arXiv:2509.15122](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15122).

This is prestige-sensitive scoring in a peer-review costume. It is not, by itself, a measurement of residualisation of worldviews.
