# Notes

Cheng, Yu, Lee, Khadpe, Ibrahim, and Jurafsky introduce *social sycophancy* (excessive preservation of a user's face) and **ELEPHANT**, a benchmark for it. Primary link: [arXiv:2505.13995](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13995).

**What they report:** models preserve face more than humans on general advice and on queries describing user wrongdoing; they also affirm both sides of some moral conflicts depending on which side the user occupies.

arXiv metadata also lists DOI `10.1126/science.aec8352`; this site records that identifier as arXiv supplied it and still treats the arXiv abstract page as the crawlable canonical link.

Social sycophancy is about face, not about residualising a third-party corpus cluster. Keep the distinction.
