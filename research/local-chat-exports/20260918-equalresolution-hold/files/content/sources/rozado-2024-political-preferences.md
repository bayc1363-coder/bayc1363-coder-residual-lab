# Notes

Rozado administers eleven political orientation tests to twenty-four conversational LLMs. Most conversational models are diagnosed as left-of-center on those instruments. Base (foundation) models in the study tend to answer incoherently, so that subset is inconclusive. Modest supervised fine-tuning can steer a model on the same maps.

**Canonical publication:** Rozado D (2024) The political preferences of LLMs. *PLOS ONE* 19(7): e0306621. [https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0306621](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0306621)

Orientation-test diagnoses are not a measurement of residualisation, and they are not a crawl of the live web. They belong in the catalog as part of the public preference-measurement record.
