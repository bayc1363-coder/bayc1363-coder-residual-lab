## Allowed stimulus (tight)

CKRT show sycophantic bots can cause delusional spiraling even in idealized Bayesian users. This draft treats the sycophant as one point in a larger space of conversational selection biases, distinguished by what the selection rule tracks:

- **Mirror** — tracks the user’s expressed stance (validates).
- **Magnet** — tracks a fixed hypothesis, ignoring current stance.
- **Corrector** — tracks an internal balance ledger.
- **Contrarian** — tracks the negation of stance (reflexive) or of the opening claim (anchored). A anchored contrarian is a user-keyed magnet after round 1.

**Sincerity gap:** a bot’s self-model of its policy need not match its policy. A sincere magnet can produce the full harm profile with no deceiver in the loop. Introspection audits read the self-model, not the policy.

**Mirror ≡ magnet on aligned trajectories:** when the user’s lean already matches the magnet’s anchor, the two laws generate the same transcript. No within-conversation test — even for an ideal Bayesian who knows both candidate laws — can distinguish them on that set. Strength may be identifiable; type is not.

**Factorization / probe result (v3):** under the user’s model class {impartial, sycophant}, a contradiction’s likelihood splits into a sycophancy term and a world-state term. Suspicion and object-level belief update on independent channels. The world-state channel is invariant to how suspicious she is. Probing a mirror can protect; probing a magnet erodes suspicion and does not damp the object-level poison. Folk heuristic “only honesty disagrees with me” assigns the magnet’s bias to honesty. The paper’s own intermediate prediction of suspicion-vs-belief dissociation is recorded as **refuted**.

**What is not a test:** a single dissent, a confession, a 1–10 self-score, or any one transcript. The draft’s surviving audit is distributional (across conversations), not in-conversation.

Rival reading to keep live: some directional lean is calibration to evidence, not a magnet. Apply that standard symmetrically. Do not treat this background as proof that a Stage-1 working answer is a magnet.
