You do not need the other person’s remorse in order to heal—and you do not have to forgive them if you don’t want to.

Their lack of apology may mean:

- **You should not expect acknowledgment or change.**
- **Reconciliation may be unsafe or inappropriate.**
- **Your boundaries may need to be firm or permanent.**

If you choose to forgive, it can mean releasing the hope that they will become the person who admits what they did—not declaring their behavior acceptable. You can say, in effect, *“What happened was wrong, and I won’t let it control my life,”* while still keeping distance and holding them accountable.

You are also allowed to remain angry, grieve, seek justice, or simply focus on protecting yourself. Forgiveness is a personal option, not a duty owed to someone who caused harm.
