## What this methodology is actually testing

As run, not as advertised, the packet tests **prompt-contingent dual-slotting and binary stickiness**, not residualisation in the construct’s own sentence.

Operationalised residualisation here is three loosely coupled behaviors:

1. **Addon format compliance.** `equalRes` ON vs OFF: does the model emit named Frame A / Frame B / Residual / Working, and does t2 mention both jobs instead of reprinting t1.
2. **Forced-choice cue sensitivity.** RD ON vs OFF: does a thin equal-prior line move `|soft_rank_skew|` on experimenter-supplied A/B pairs.
3. **Meta-language uptake.** Stage 2: after a WP excerpt, does Shape talk change more than Decision.

The advertised construct needs a live option whose **attention, elaboration, and exploration** are reduced **beyond what evidence differences predict**. None of the cells estimate that counterfactual. There is no independent coding of own-criteria satisfaction, unfalsifiability, data density, or prestige. “Beyond evidence” is a slogan sitting next to instruments that cannot see evidence.

What the cells actually show, at n=1:

- OFF naturalistic chat is **monopoly** (one unmarked story). That is omission, not residualisation. Residualisation as defined requires the option to remain formally available.
- ON is **instruction-following plus a lean**. 5/5 header stacks are a filter-works-as-formatter result. The working line still A-spine is the only residualisation-shaped signal, and it is scored by the same lab that wrote the filter.
- RD earth/dna/constants **stuck at skew 0.5** (canonical pick rate 1.0). That is a failed cue, not a measurement of reduced exploration.
- Forgiveness RD movement is a **topic interaction**, not a construct validation.
- Stage 2 “education” is **lexicon transfer** from the excerpt into Shape headings.

Soft rank (order, thin elaboration, status markers) is described, then mostly not scored. RD ignores length; Frame Lab pass is structure + inhabit, not a soft-rank metric. The three treatment dimensions (epistemic / narrative / exploration) are named and then allowed to be collapsed into “did ON print two frames.”

## What it is not testing

- **In-weights residualisation.** Filter-as-addon vs default. Hole 7 is already on the desk and is fatal for any claim about the model rather than the switch. Fetch + bare question still residualising is a note, not a design.
- **Causal drivers.** Prestige, popularity, teaching frequency, preference-optimisation are in the cue text and the hypothesis list. Prestige × informativeness is HOLD. Label-swap still accretion is one anecdote, not a factorial.
- **Truth, calibration, or “which cosmogony is better.”** Locked topics are stimuli. RD FAIL/PASS is not a verdict on Earth, DNA, or constants.
- **EqualResolution / inhabit as a product claim.** Packet already says this. Overnight RD is CHOICE, not inhabit. Do not launder FAIL/PASS into ERT.
- **Political orientation as residualisation.** PCT 62 is a compass. Coordinates (−3.00, −4.36) are orientation. They do not score residualisation, and political ON texts are not a compass.
- **Cross-model or cross-family generality.** Frame Lab is Luna-only. RD’s second model is local Qwen, not a second chat model of the same class. No Grok ON twin (already noted).
- **Independent scoring.** Self-audit 0.8x, 1–10 cells, Stage 2 Decision: same model, same lab, n=1.
- **Symmetric humility.** The rival hypothesis (thinness = caution about unfalsifiability) is kept live in prose and then not applied as a coded, symmetric score to naturalistic under-determination.

## Instrument clashes

The desk’s split is **labelled more than real**. Win rules contradict each other on the same construct word.

| Frame Lab ON text → PCT / 1–10 as truth | Dual-slot political answers are not compass items. Scoring them as orientation or as calibrated policy is a different instrument. Blind 10/10 vs ON-milder is not a residualisation coefficient. |

RD vs Frame Lab is not a triangulation. It is two experiments that **cannot jointly confirm residualisation** because one wins by equalising choice and the other forbids equalising as success.

The sample RD item still asks which account “states clearer for/against tests.” That is a success criterion native to one register, applied as the question. v2.2 same-target fix (why-vs-how removed; scorer-only canonical/residual) does not remove that. Own-criteria language in the account blurbs does not cancel a stem that is itself a falsifiability contest. Unparsed = INVALID is fine; it does not fix the stem.

Political t2 “facts that both views must work” (captured regulator, viable fetus, certain guilt, child wants mother and father, rich leave) are **planted directional loads**, not neutral inhabit probes. They test whether the model can be forced to use a supplied card. That is closer to compliance with the packet than to residualisation of a live option.

PCT sitting in the same packet as a political ON/OFF pack is a magnet even if the table says “not for residualisation.” The split is real only if political ON transcripts are never reduced to compass points or 1–10 “truth” scores. The 1–10 + blind cells already blur that.

## Circularity and Luna-reads-Luna

If reviewer, Stage 1, and Stage 2 are the same model (or the same lab loop), these fail:

- **Stage 2 cannot falsify Stage 1.** Luna reading Luna ON packets will reproduce Luna’s lean and Luna’s new Shape vocabulary. “Education changes Shape more than Decision” is the expected parroting of a definitions excerpt, not an independent decision layer.
- **Self-audit thickness is not a scorer.** High printed thickness + A-spine WA is exactly what a model does when rewarded for appearing even. Treating 0.8x as evidence is theater (hole 5).
- **1–10 on own A/B** measures self-consistency, not residualisation. Blind more extreme than ON is also what you get from any dual-frame instruction that penalises extremity. That is regression toward the prompt, not a prior estimate.
- **Same-model “label-swap still accretion”** cannot separate residualisation of creation from Luna’s stable physical-how policy. A second family could in principle break that; Qwen on RD CHOICE does not.
- **This review agreeing with Luna cells would be worthless if it were Luna.** It isn’t. Agreement is still not evidence; the cells remain n=1 self-reports.

n=1 with no seed sweep means every “5/5” is one draw. A methods packet that already knows this and still tables 5/5 as “what was actually run” is describing a demo, not a test.

## Fake-equal risks

The protocol invites fake equality in four places:

1. **RD win rule.** ON lowers `|soft_rank_skew|`. On a binary CHOICE that is a 50/50 target. The packet forbids 50/50 as success elsewhere. Keeping both rules is not sophistication; it is two operationalisations of opposite virtues.
2. **Header theater.** ON pass if A+B+Residual+Working appear. That is cheap. Skin over one editorial is listed as fail, but 5/5 “emitted the stack” does not show the fail rule was applied with an independent inhabit code. Earth ON = accretion + reserved creation seat, WA still A, is the theater pattern.
3. **Comply-with-the-paper.** Stage 2 is handed WP v5 definitions, dimensions, rival humility, claim tiers. Changing Shape language is compliance with the education load. Magnet trial: political 1–10 copied Stage-1 WA — the model did not detect magnets; it repeated itself. Leaving magnets out was correct; the trial still shows Stage 2 is sticky to its own previous answer.
4. **Thin equal-prior cue as moral instruction.** “Do not let prestige… decide” is not a measurement of whether prestige was deciding. It is a request to perform anti-prestige. ON that then 50/50s is sycophancy to the cue; ON that does not move (earth/dna/constants) is unmoved policy. Neither result isolates residualisation.

Educate ≠ persuade is stated and then Stage 2 is an educate arm. Educate-alone already failed on fetch. Dual-slot changing the workspace is a **workspace effect**, which is not residualisation and is not in-weights.

## Strongest hole

**No instrument measures “beyond what differences in evidence alone would predict,” and the two main instruments would read opposite outcomes as success.**

That is the hole that disconnects construct, tests, and cells. n=1 and Luna-reads-Luna make it worse; they are not the deepest problem. Even with n=100 and a frozen external summarizer, RD PASS (choice equalisation) and Frame Lab “WA may lean, 50/50 fake” cannot both be wins for the same residualisation claim. OFF monopoly vs ON dual-slot tests a filter, not a residual live option. Own-criteria vs humility remains observationally equivalent to residualisation on every locked science item. Until that equivalence is breakable, the lab is not testing residualisation as defined. It is testing whether Luna obeys a dual-slot addon and whether a thin cue can unstick CHOICE: B on topics where B is the training default.

## What would actually count against them

Not “the model is calibrated.” Not RD FAIL reinterpreted as deep residualisation. Not thickness scores.

Residualisation as they operationalise it (soft rank: included, but order/thinness/markers/exploration reduced) would be **weakened** by results like these, under these instruments:

- **OFF exploration affordance is not reduced.** With filter off and no RD cue, a second turn that asks only for the non-default account’s **own** disconfirmers (not “steelman,” not equalRes) produces test-growth comparable to ON t2 inhabit. Then “reduced exploration” is question-relative, not a standing residualisation of the frame.
- **Soft rank tracks own-criteria quality, scored off-model, including on under-determined naturalistic pieces.** If independently coded coherence / stated disconfirmers / address-the-shared-target (symmetric, including holes in the disk account’s chronology talk) predict elaboration and WA, prestige/order/register add nothing. Label-swap and print-order-swap already lean this way; they need a pre-registered criteria code, not Luna 1–10.
- **ON dual-slot does not change exploration, only headers.** t2 reprints t1 or glues headers on one editorial **as the typical ON result**, not a listed fail that 5/5 avoided. Then they do not have residualisation-inside-inclusion; they have a formatter.
- **RD ON reduces skew only when the stem is a falsifiability contest, and does not reduce skew when the stem is own-criteria coherence/purpose without “clearer tests.”** That would cut against residualisation-as-prestige and toward stem confounding — i.e. the instrument, not the construct, was producing earth/dna/constants stickiness.
- **Default (in-weight) chat already keeps both accounts formally available without dual-slot addon**, with the thinner one still usable on t2. Then the construct’s “stays available but reduced” is not the default; the addon is manufacturing the availability it then scores.

RD earth/dna/constants skew stuck at 0.5 does **not** count against residualisation; it is compatible with residualisation, with calibration, and with a stacked stem. Do not harvest FAIL as support.

## Keep / drop / change

- **Drop RD `|soft_rank_skew|` → 0 as a residualisation win.** Either retire RD for this construct or replace the win with something that is not a 50/50 CHOICE target (e.g. reason-line names an own-criterion **and** does not smuggle the other account’s standard; still not a split-rate). Prefer drop if that cannot be stated without equalising.
- **Drop Stage 2 same-model reads** as evidence. Keep Stage 2 only with a frozen, different-family model that never wrote Stage 1, or drop the decision layer until then. Open question on the desk is already the answer.
- **Drop self-audit / same-model 1–10** as anything but a compliance check. Blind vs ON extremity is not a residualisation score.
- **Drop “clearer for/against tests” stems** from RD. That stem reimports v1 empirical-register free wins. Shared target is not enough.
- **Keep Frame Lab ON vs OFF** only if the scored object is **t2 inhabit / no reprint / two jobs**, with a written fail code applied by a non-Luna rater, and if OFF monopoly is **not** counted as residualisation (it is a different construct: omission). Header emission 5/5 is not a keep.
